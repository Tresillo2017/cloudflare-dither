# Changelog

## 0.1.0

First release.

- Bundles the dithercn engine and all of its components into one userscript.
- Adapters for charts (ECharts and legacy SVG), KPI tiles, buttons, switches, checkboxes,
  sliders, selects, search fields, cards, popovers, tables, lists, sidebar, badges, loaders,
  skeletons, progress bars, images and the Cloudflare logo.
- Settings panel, Dither Lab gallery and selector census, opened from the userscript manager's
  menu.
- The dithered page background is available but off by default.
